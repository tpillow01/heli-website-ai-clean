Sales Process

New leads begin in Homework unless a later stage is selected above.

1. Homework
Research the company, facility, contacts, fleet, expansion project, and any information that will help prepare for the first meaningful conversation.
2. Rapport
Establish credibility and begin building a relationship with the right people.
3. Pain Point
Understand the operational issues, frustrations, downtime, cost, or problems the customer wants solved.
4. Buying Process
Identify decision makers, approval requirements, budget timing, purchasing steps, and expected timeline.
5. Solve Pain Points
Determine exactly how Tynan and HELI can address the customer's identified needs.
6. Proposal
Build and present the right equipment, service, financial, and support solution.
7. Close
Identify what remains before the customer can commit and create a clear path to the final decision.
8. Lost
If the opportunity is lost, document exactly why so the team can learn from the outcome.
